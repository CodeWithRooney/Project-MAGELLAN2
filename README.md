# Athena

## Project Overview

Athena is a career guidance platform designed to help students make informed decisions about their education and careers. Many students are unaware of the opportunities available to them, such as different career paths, scholarships, universities, competitions, and personalized learning roadmaps. Athena aims to provide all this information in one platform, along with personalized recommendations based on each user's interests, goals, and profile.

---

## Problem Statement

Many students struggle to choose the right career due to a lack of proper guidance and reliable resources. Information about careers, scholarships, higher education opportunities, competitions, and skill development is often scattered across multiple websites, making it difficult to explore and compare options. As a result, students may miss valuable opportunities or make uninformed career decisions.

---

## Proposed Solution

Athena provides a centralized and personalized platform that assists students throughout their career journey. After creating an account and completing their profile, users receive recommendations tailored to their interests, academic background, skills, and career aspirations.

The platform will help users:
- Explore different career options.
- Discover scholarships and financial aid opportunities.
- Search for universities and higher education programs.
- Find competitions, hackathons, Olympiads, and internships.
- Access personalized learning roadmaps.
- Receive AI-assisted guidance for career planning.

---

## Features

### User Authentication
- Secure user registration and login.
- Password encryption for account security.

### Personalized Dashboard
- Customized experience based on user profile and interests.

### Career Exploration
- Explore a wide range of career paths.
- Detailed information about each career.

### Scholarships
- Search and filter scholarship opportunities.

### Universities
- Explore universities and their programs.

### Competitions
- Discover hackathons, Olympiads, internships, and other opportunities.

### Learning Roadmaps
- Personalized roadmaps to help users achieve their career goals.

### AI Assistance
- Intelligent recommendations based on user preferences and career objectives.
